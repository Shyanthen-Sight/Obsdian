## Ⅲ. 句子翻译（每小题 3 分）

**51.** Tom and I are good friends, and we are in the same class.
> 并列主语用 are；I 放在并列主语最后。

**52.** My sister is doing her homework in her room now. She does her homework every evening.
> 第一句 now 提示现在进行时；第二句 every evening 提示一般现在时，主语三单用 does。

**53.** This card is for me, and that schoolbag is hers.
> 介词 for 后用宾格 me；句末无名词，用名词性物主代词 hers。

**54.** There is a big blackboard and twenty-four desks in our new classroom.
> there be 就近原则：紧跟的是 a big blackboard（单数）→ is。

**55.** My brother can play the piano, but he can't play football.
> 乐器前加 the，球类前不加冠词；can / can't 后接动词原形。

**56.** He gets up at 6:30 every day, but he is still sleeping now.
> 前半句 every day 用一般现在时三单 gets；后半句 now 用现在进行时。

**57.** January is the first month of the year, and my birthday is on January 1st.
> 序数词 first 前加 the；具体某天（日期）前用 on。

**58.** Today is Children's Day, and the children are all very happy.
> Children's Day 用不规则复数 children 的所有格；第二空 the children 作主语，用 are。

**59.** My English teacher is an honest person, and she often helps us.
> honest 以元音音素开头，用 an；help 后用宾格 us。

**60.** The girl in the red dress is my cousin, and her English is very good.
> 「穿……的」用介词短语 in the red dress 作后置定语；修饰 English 用 her。

## 学生易错点提醒（供讲评用）

1. **并列主语三原则**：`and / both...and` 用 are；`neither...nor / either...or / not only...but also / there be` 就近；`as well as / together with / besides / but` 就远。学生最容易「看谁近就用谁」。
2. **不可数名词的干扰项**：`The water in the two bottles` 中的 two bottles 只是修饰语，主语中心词是 water。
3. **状态动词不能用进行时**：like / want / know / have（有）— 句首出现 Look! 时尤其容易全线误判。
4. **冠词听音不听字母**：an honest boy、a university student、a useful book、a European country。
5. **序数词拼写**：fifth、eighth、ninth、twelfth、twentieth、twenty-first 是高频错点。
6. **序数词前 the 的省略**：有物主代词或名词所有格时不再加 the（my twelfth birthday）。
7. **do / does 一出现，动词立刻回原形**：Does your brother go...? / He doesn't go...
8. **there be 的否定与疑问不借助 do**：Is there a park near here?（不是 Does there be）
