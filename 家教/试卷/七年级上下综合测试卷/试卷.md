## Ⅰ. 单项选择（共 15 小题，每小题 2 分，满分 30 分）

从 A、B、C、D 四个选项中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。

**1.** Neither Tom nor his parents ________ at home last night, so nobody opened the door for me.
A. were　B. was　C. are　D. is

**2.** Li Ming is a good friend of ________. He often helps ________ with ________ English.
A. mine; me; my　B. my; me; mine　C. mine; I; my　D. mine; me; mine

**3.** These ________ are mine, but those ________ on the desk are ________.
A. keys; knifes; Lily's　B. keyes; knives; Lilys'　C. keys; knives; Lily's　D. keys; knives; Lilys

**4.** There ________ some bread and three eggs on the table, and there ________ a bottle of milk next to them.
A. are; are　B. is; are　C. are; is　D. is; is

**5.** — Does your brother ________ to school by bike?
　　　— No, he ________. He ________ on foot.
A. goes; doesn't; goes　B. go; doesn't; goes　C. go; don't; goes　D. go; doesn't; go

**6.** Look! The girl in red ________ happy, and she ________ her new dress very much.
A. looks; likes　B. is looking; likes　C. looks; is liking　D. is looking; is liking

**7.** Listen! The girls ________ in the classroom, and their teacher ________ at the door.
A. are singing; is standing　B. is singing; is standing　C. are singing; stands　D. sing; is standing

**8.** Lily can play ________ guitar, and she often plays ________ basketball with ________ honest boy.
A. the; the; an　B. the; /; an　C. /; /; a　D. the; /; a

**9.** Today is my sister's ________ birthday. She was born on the ________ of December.
A. twelfth; twentyth　B. twelve; twentieth　C. twelfth; twentieth　D. twelveth; twentieth

**10.** Two bottles of milk ________ on the desk, and a bag of rice ________ in the kitchen. A pair of shoes ________ under the bed.
A. are; is; is　B. is; is; are　C. are; are; is　D. are; is; are

**11.** There are five ________ students in our school, and ________ birds fly over the lake every year.
A. hundred; thousands of　B. hundreds; thousands of　C. hundred; thousand of　D. hundreds; thousand

**12.** The bank is ________ the post office and the supermarket; the bookstore is ________ from the school.
A. among; in front　B. between; across　C. between; in front　D. among; across

**13.** — ________ he ________ the old man cross the road yesterday?　— Yes, he did.
A. Did; help　B. Did; helped　C. Does; help　D. Was; helping

**14.** — Must I hand in the homework today?
　　　— No, you ________. You can hand it in tomorrow.
A. mustn't　B. needn't　C. can't　D. shouldn't

**15.** He speaks English very ________, and he is also a ________ boy.
A. well; friendly　B. good; friendly　C. well; friend　D. good; friend

## Ⅴ. 阅读理解（共 10 小题，每小题 2 分，满分 20 分）

阅读下列短文，从每小题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中，选出最佳选项。

### A

Last Friday was my birthday. I am thirteen now. Early in the morning, my mother said to me, "Don't be late for school today, Tom." I was a little surprised — it was my birthday!

When I got to school, my friends said nothing about my birthday. I felt sad and thought, "They are not my real friends."

In the afternoon, we had a science class. Our teacher, Mr Green, taught us about the rain. Then the last class ended. I walked slowly to the school gate.

Suddenly, all my friends came out and shouted, "Surprise! Happy birthday!" There was a big cake and a card on a desk. The card had all their names on it. I was so happy that I could say nothing.

My best friend Li Ming said, "We did this for you. We hope you like it." I said, "Thank you! This is the best birthday."

I learned something important that day: real friends are always there for you, even when they are quiet.
