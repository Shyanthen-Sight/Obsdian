## 一、情绪、心理、状态类

1. 疲惫、压力相关

- be stressed out = be worn out 疲惫不堪
- wear out 磨损；使精疲力竭
- put stress on 强调；给… 施加负担
- beat up (被动) 被工作折磨、心力交瘁
- drag oneself to sp 疲惫地慢慢走向某地

2. 渴望、追求、决心

- be starving for sth 渴望某物；be starving to do 渴望做
- be greedy for sth /be greedy to do 渴求；贪求
- seize a chance/an opportunity 抓住机会
- be resolved to do = make a resolution to do 下定决心做
- aspire after sth 追求、向往某物
- feel impelled to do 不得不做、被迫做
- ramp up efforts to do 加大力度去做

3. 热爱、感兴趣、投入沉浸

- immerse oneself in (doing) sth /be immersed in 沉浸于
- have a penchant for /have a keen interest in /be fascinated by 对… 感兴趣
- be enthusiastic about (doing) sth = have passion for doing
    
    = be obsessed with doing = have an obsession with doing 对… 满怀热情、痴迷
- enthusiastic adj. 热情的；enthusiasm n. 热情；with enthusiasm = enthusiastically 热情地

4. 羡慕、嫉妒

- envy sb sth 羡慕某人某物；envy sb doing sth 羡慕某人做某事
- with envy = enviously 羡慕地；out of envy 出于嫉妒
- be green with envy 嫉妒得眼红
- be envious of sth 羡慕某物
- envier n. 羡慕者

5. 低落、心情沉重

- one’s heart/spirits sink 心情低落、情绪消沉
- feel in no mood to do sth 没心情做某事

6. 同情、包容、容忍

- tolerate (sb) doing sth 容忍（某人）做某事
- be tolerant of/towards sb/sth = put up with (sb doing) 宽容、忍受
- be/feel sympathetic to/towards/with 同情、赞同、支持

7. 放松、释怀

- lighten up 放松、别生气

## 二、动作、行为、人际、处事
1. 付出时间精力

- expend one’s time/labor/effort/endeavor in doing 花费时间精力做
- endeavor to do = make endeavors to do 努力、尽力做
- make a point of doing sth 特意、重视做某事

2. 帮助、携手合作

- reach out a helping hand to 向… 伸出援手；reach out 伸出手
- pool effort/labor 齐心协力、共同努力
- shore up 支撑、加固

3. 负责、确保

- sb see to sth 照料、负责某事
- sb see to it that… 确保、务必…

4. 坚持、顶住

- persist in doing 坚持做；persist through 经受住；persist despite 尽管… 仍坚持

5. 出面介入、退让

- step in 介入、插手
- back off 退让、退出

6. 代表、发声演讲

- on behalf of sb 代表某人
- stand for = represent sb 代表
- lecture about 就… 发表演讲
- deliver an online lecture on the theme of… 发表主题线上讲座

7. 上街游行、抗议

- take to the street 走上街头

8. 充当、作为

- serve as = act as 充当、担任

9. 折中方案

- compromise approach 折中方法

## 三、感官、思绪、记忆、表象

1. 思绪、回忆

- Flash through one's mind 念头在脑海闪过
- bring/call…to mind 使想起

2. 面部神态、气色、光影染色

- wear an expression of… 面带… 神情
- There was a flush in her cheeks. 她满脸通红
- The setting sun flushed the mountain tops. 落日染红山头

3. 看待、视作

- perceive as 把… 看作

4. 表明、意味

- it signifies that… 这说明、这表明

## 四、变化、增减、消逝、磨损、结果
1. 增减、延长、超出

- increase by 增加了；increase to 增加到
- extend by 延长了；extend to 延长到
- exceed in 在某方面出众；exceed by 超出…；exceed expectation 超出预期

2. 磨损、消逝、衰退

- wear away 磨损；时光消逝；精力衰退

3. 最终、结果

- turn out to be (a huge success) 结果是（大获成功）
- end up doing 最终以做… 收场
- on cue 恰好在这时、恰逢其时

4. 提升、加强

- polish up 提升、精进
- ramp up 提升、增加
- move up 提升、提前、靠前

5. 涌出、涌现

- well over 涌出（well v. 涌出；n. 水井）
