## Ⅰ. 单项选择（共 25 小题，每小题 1 分，满分 25 分）

从 A、B、C、D 四个选项中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。

**1.** — ________ Jack and his brother in Grade Seven?
　　　— No, they ________. They are in Grade Eight.
A. Is; aren't　B. Are; aren't　C. Are; isn't　D. Is; isn't

**2.** Neither the students nor their teacher ________ in the classroom now.
A. are　B. be　C. is　D. am

**3.** The water in the two bottles ________ not enough, but the bread on the plate ________ fresh.
A. are; is　B. is; are　C. are; are　D. is; is

**4.** Tom, as well as his friends, ________ from Canada.
A. is　B. are　C. am　D. be

**5.** — Is this ________ pen?　— No, it's not ________. ________ is in the pencil box.
A. your; my; My　B. your; mine; Mine　C. yours; mine; Mine　D. your; mine; My

**6.** Li Ming is a good friend of ________. He often helps ________ with ________ English.
A. my; I; my　B. mine; I; my　C. me; me; mine　D. mine; me; my

**7.** These ________ are mine, but those ________ on the desk are ________.
A. keys; knifes; Lily's　B. keyes; knives; Lilys'　C. keys; knives; Lily's　D. keys; knives; Lilys

**8.** — Whose pen is this? Is it ________?
　　　— No, it's not ________ pen. ________ pen is in her bag.
A. hers; she; She　B. her; hers; Her　C. hers; her; Her　D. hers; her; She

**9.** There ________ some bread and three eggs on the table, and there ________ a bottle of milk next to them.
A. are; are　B. is; are　C. are; is　D. is; is

**10.** My uncle ________ a big farm, and ________ many cows and sheep on it.
A. has; there is　B. have; there are　C. has; there are　D. has; it has

**11.** — Does your brother ________ to school by bike?
　　　— No, he ________. He ________ on foot.
A. goes; doesn't; goes　B. go; doesn't; goes　C. go; don't; goes　D. go; doesn't; go

**12.** There ________ not any water in the glass, and there ________ not any apples in the basket, either.
A. is; is　B. are; are　C. is; are　D. are; is

**13.** This present is for ________, and the card is from ________.
A. you and I; they　B. you and me; they　C. you and I; them　D. you and me; them

**14.** Listen! Someone ________ an English song in the next room.
A. sings　B. are singing　C. is singing　D. sing

**15.** Look! The girl in red ________ happy, and she ________ her new dress very much.
A. looks; likes　B. is looking; likes　C. looks; is liking　D. is looking; is liking

**16.** — What ________ your brother ________ now?
　　　— He ________ to watch TV, but he ________ a lot of homework.
A. does; do; wants; has　B. is; doing; wants; has　C. is; doing; is wanting; has　D. is; do; wants; has

**17.** Please be quiet. The baby ________ now, and his parents ________ about his birthday party in the next room.
A. sleeps; talk　B. is sleeping; is talking　C. is sleeping; are talking　D. is sleeping; talks

**18.** My brother is ________ honest boy, and he wants to be ________ university student in ________ European country.
A. a; a; a　B. an; a; a　C. an; an; a　D. an; a; an

**19.** I can play ________ guitar, but I can't play ________ basketball. My brother likes playing ________ chess.
A. the; the; /　B. /; the; /　C. the; /; the　D. the; /; /

**20.** There is ________ elephant and ________ horse in the zoo. ________ elephant is from Africa.
A. an; a; The　B. a; a; The　C. an; a; An　D. an; an; The

**21.** She can ________ three languages, but she can't ________ Chinese well.
A. speaks; writes　B. speak; write　C. speak; writes　D. speaks; write

**22.** There are ________ floors in this building, and I live on the ________ floor.
A. twelve; twelve　B. twelve; twelfth　C. twelfth; twelve　D. twelfth; twelfth

**23.** December is the ________ month of the year, and my birthday is on the ________ of December.
A. twelve; twentieth　B. twelfth; twentyth　C. twelfth; twentieth　D. twelveth; twentieth

**24.** We'll have a party ________ the evening of June 1st, and the party starts ________ seven. We'll sing and dance ________ the evening.
A. in; at; in　B. on; in; at　C. at; at; in　D. on; at; in

**25.** — What is your father doing?
　　　— He ________ newspapers. He usually ________ them after supper, but now he ________ a magazine.
A. is reading; reads; is reading　B. reads; reads; is reading　C. is reading; read; is reading　D. is reading; reads; reads

## Ⅳ. 阅读理解（共 10 小题，每小题 2 分，满分 20 分）

阅读下列短文，从每小题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中，选出最佳选项。

### A

Hi, I'm Tony. I'm from Canada. I'm thirteen years old. Now I am a student in Class Two, Grade Seven. I have a good friend. His name is Li Ming. We are in the same class. Li Ming is good at English, and he often helps me with it. We both like sports. Look! We are playing basketball on the playground now. We are having a good time.

**61.** Tony and Li Ming are ________.
A. in different classes　B. in the same class　C. in Grade Eight　D. from Canada

**62.** In the sentence "he often helps me with **it**", "it" refers to ________.
A. basketball　B. the class　C. English　D. Canada

### B

Look at this photo. It is a photo of my family. The man in the black coat is my father. He is a doctor. The tall woman is my mother. She is a teacher, and she teaches English. The girl with long hair is my sister, Lily. She is only six years old. We have two cats. Their names are Mimi and Coco. They are playing with a ball now. My family is not big, but we are very happy.

**63.** What does the writer's mother do?
A. She is a doctor.　B. She is a nurse.　C. She is a worker.　D. She is a teacher.

**64.** Which of the following is TRUE?
A. The writer's father is a teacher.
B. The writer's mother teaches Chinese.
C. Lily is the writer's sister, and she is six years old.
D. The family has only one cat, and it is playing with a ball.

### C

Our school is very big. There are three buildings in it. In the first building, there is a big library and two computer rooms. The library has a lot of books and it is very quiet. In the second building, there are twenty-four classrooms. Our classroom is on the third floor. It is clean and bright. There is a blackboard on the front wall and forty desks in it. In the third building, there are some science labs. We have science lessons there every Tuesday. I like my school very much.

**65.** Where is the writer's classroom?
A. On the third floor.　B. In the first building.　C. In the science lab.　D. In the library.

**66.** What is / are there in the writer's classroom?
A. A blackboard and forty desks.　B. Two computer rooms.　C. A lot of books.　D. Some science labs.

### D

Pandas are my favourite animals. They are black and white, and they look very cute. A big panda is about 1.5 metres tall. Pandas live in the forests in Sichuan, China. They eat bamboo every day — about 30 kilos a day! Pandas can climb trees, and they can swim, too. But baby pandas are very small. A baby panda is only about 100 grams. Look at the picture! A baby panda is sleeping in its mother's arms. It looks so happy.

**67.** What can we learn about pandas from the passage?
A. Pandas eat about 30 kilos of bamboo a day.　B. Pandas are white and brown.　C. Pandas can't swim.　D. Pandas live in the sea.

**68.** What is the baby panda doing in the picture?
A. It is eating bamboo.　B. It is climbing a tree.　C. It is swimming.　D. It is sleeping in its mother's arms.

### E

Today is September 10th — Teachers' Day in China. It is a special day for all teachers. In the morning, we have a class meeting. We say "Happy Teachers' Day!" to our teachers. Then we give cards and flowers to them. My classmates and I make the cards together. On each card, we write, "Thank you, Miss Wang!" Our English teacher, Miss Wang, is very happy. She says, "Thank you, boys and girls. I like your cards very much."

**69.** When is Teachers' Day in China?
A. On September 1st.　B. On October 1st.　C. On September 10th.　D. On March 8th.

**70.** Where do the students write "Thank you, Miss Wang!"?
A. On the flowers.　B. On the cards.　C. On the blackboard.　D. In their books.
